Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

FairShare Club ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the FairShare Club mobile application and web application (collectively, the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Account information: Email address and display name when you sign in via Google or email authentication.
  • Group data: Group names, member lists, and group settings you create within the App.
  • Expense data: Expense descriptions, amounts, payer information, and split details you enter.
  • Settlement data: Payment records between group members that you log within the App.
  • Device information: Basic device and usage data collected automatically by Firebase for app performance monitoring.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App's core features (expense splitting, group management, balance tracking).
  • Enable real-time synchronisation of data across devices within your groups.
  • Send in-app notifications related to group activity (expenses added, settlements marked).
  • Improve and optimise the App's performance and user experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase Firestore (Cloud Firestore), a cloud database provided by Google LLC. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We implement Firebase Security Rules to ensure only authorised group members can access group data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data may be shared:

  • Within your group: Group members can see expenses, balances, and settlements within shared groups.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (data storage and authentication). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

5. Authentication

FairShare Club uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in. We support email/password and Google Sign-In. We do not store passwords — authentication is managed entirely by Google Firebase.

6. Children's Privacy

FairShare Club is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data via the App's Settings → Clear Data, or by contacting us.
  • Export your expense data using the export feature within the App.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your data at any time from within the App (Settings → Clear All Data). Upon deletion, all your groups, expenses, and personal data are permanently removed from our servers.

9. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase – Authentication, database, and hosting
  • Google Fonts – Typography

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
