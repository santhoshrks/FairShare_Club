Delete Your FairShare Club Account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from within the FairShare Club app, or by contacting us via email.

Option 1 — Delete from within the App

Follow these steps to delete your account and data:

1. Open the FairShare Club app on your device.
2. Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) on the home screen.
3. Scroll down to "Clear All Data".
4. Tap "Clear All Data" and confirm when prompted.
5. All your groups, expenses, settlements, and personal data will be permanently deleted.

Option 2 — Request via Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at:
📧 support@fairshare-club.web.app

Please include the email address associated with your FairShare Club account. We will process your request within 7 business days.

What data is deleted?

🗑️ Permanently deleted:

  • Your account profile (name, email)
  • All groups you created
  • All expenses you added
  • All settlements and payment records
  • All contributions and wallet transactions
  • Your saved contacts within the app

📋 What happens to shared group data:

  • Groups you created and shared with others will also be deleted.
  • Other members of your groups will no longer see the shared data.
⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

Delete only your data (without deleting account)

If you want to clear your data without fully deleting your account:

1. Open the app → Settings → "Clear All Data".
2. This removes all your groups and expenses while keeping your account active.
